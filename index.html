About R.M. Vision Academy

Bridging traditional academic excellence with laboratory geoinformatics

Our Vision

To build an intellectually vibrant ecosystem at R.M. Vision Academy where every Geography enthusiast attains mastery over fundamental physical and human spatial dynamics while acquiring industry-ready GIS, remote sensing, and research computing capabilities.

Our Mission

To deliver high-impact, affordable, and mentor-guided instruction. Under the academic leadership of Rohamat Mandal (BA Aliah University, MA JNU New Delhi, UGC-NET), we bridge academic curricula with practical laboratory workflows: cartography, spatial modeling, hypothesis testing, and satellite analytics for competitive excellence.
